# Code of conduct

Treat people with respect. Welcome newcomers, listen to different views, and give feedback about the work rather than the person. Respect privacy and use inclusive language.

Harassment, discrimination, sexual attention, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable in this project's spaces.

For abuse on GitHub, use [GitHub's private reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not post sensitive reports in an issue. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or block an account. They will keep reports private to the extent they can and avoid retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.
